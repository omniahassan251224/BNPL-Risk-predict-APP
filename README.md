# 💳 BNPL Credit Risk Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL)** customer data to understand repayment behavior, identify high-risk customers, and discover the key factors associated with payment defaults.

The project combines **data analysis, risk analysis, data visualization, and business insights** to support better decision-making in BNPL financial services.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

BNPL companies face the challenge of identifying customers who are more likely to **default on their payments**.

A high default rate can lead to financial losses and increased credit risk. Therefore, analyzing customer characteristics such as **credit score, income, age, and purchase category** can help identify risk patterns and improve customer risk assessment.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze BNPL customer and transaction data.
* Identify factors associated with payment defaults.
* Measure the overall **Default Rate**.
* Identify **high-risk and low-risk customers**.
* Analyze the relationship between credit score and repayment behavior.
* Compare default rates across income, age, and product categories.
* Provide actionable business insights and recommendations.
* Build an interactive dashboard for monitoring BNPL credit risk.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

Based on the risk analysis, BNPL providers can:

* Apply stricter credit limits to high-risk customers.
* Use credit score as an important factor in risk assessment.
* Monitor customer segments with consistently high default rates.
* Adjust BNPL limits according to customer income and repayment history.
* Develop targeted strategies for customers with high-risk profiles.
* Continuously monitor default trends to reduce potential financial losses.
